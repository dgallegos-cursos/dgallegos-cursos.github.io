Research

My work is organised in two groups of lines that share tools: hydrodynamics, geometry and field theory. All six are below, with their connections.

Fluids and thermodynamics

Theories and simulations for continuous systems in the hydrodynamic limit: from the quark–gluon plasma to cell membranes and porous media.

  1. 1

    Relativistic hydrodynamics: spin and magnetic fields

    I work on the relativistic hydrodynamics of fluids with spin, of fluids in strong magnetic fields and of strongly anisotropic fluids. These theories describe systems such as the quark–gluon plasma created in heavy-ion collisions.

    Representative paper: Hydrodynamics of spin currents

    Connects with: Holography and strings (transport) · On the horizon (spin)

  2. 2

    Fluid membranes

    I describe fluid membranes, like those surrounding cells, by combining hydrodynamics, thermodynamics and differential geometry with techniques from general relativity. I want to understand the equations that govern their shape and dynamics.

    Representative paper: Hydrostatic Newton–Cartan membranes

    Connects with: Non-relativistic gravity (Newton–Cartan)

  3. 3

    Computational fluid dynamics

    I simulate complex flows, mainly with parallel computing on graphics cards (GPUs) using CUDA. The systems I study include flow in porous media, granular media, and heat transport by convection and conduction.

    Representative paper: Numerical study of the flow through porous structures built from Gray–Scott patterns

    Connects with: On the horizon (liquid metals)

Gravitation and high-energy physics

Questions about foundations: how field theories, strings and gravity are related, and what gravity looks like far from the relativistic regime.

  1. 4

    Holography and strings

    The holographic principle relates quantum field theories to theories of strings and gravity. I use it as a tool to study transport in strongly coupled fluids, and I look for its origin in matrix models, aiming to build a direct dictionary between the two descriptions.

    Representative papers: Strings from interacting quantum fields foundationsHolographic transport in anisotropic plasmas applications

    Connects with: Relativistic hydrodynamics (transport) · Non-relativistic gravity (strings)

  2. 5

    Non-relativistic gravity

    I study torsional Newton–Cartan gravity, a non-relativistic theory of gravity, from several angles: how it arises from string theory, what it describes as a theory of gravitation, and how it can serve as a simpler laboratory for exploring quantum gravity.

    Representative paper: Torsional Newton–Cartan gravity from non-relativistic strings

    Connects with: Holography and strings (strings) · Fluid membranes (Newton–Cartan) · On the horizon (torsion)

New directions

  1. 6

    On the horizon

    I explore directions where hydrodynamics and geometry meet condensed matter, such as the generation of spin currents by vorticity in liquid metals, or materials whose defects act as torsion.

    Connects with: Relativistic hydrodynamics (spin) · Computational fluid dynamics (liquid metals) · Non-relativistic gravity (torsion)
